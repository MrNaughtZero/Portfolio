Playing and accounts

You can play as a guest or choose Sign in with Apple. Keep your Apple account secure. A Gridly account lets you save progress and use account features. Guest progress may not be recoverable after deleting the app or changing devices.

Please play fairly. Do not manipulate scores, exploit purchase systems, attempt to access another player's account or use an abusive or misleading player name.

Optional purchases

Gridly offers optional consumable power-ups, Story lives refills and a 30-minute unlimited-lives pass. The price and contents are shown before Apple's purchase confirmation. Power-ups and lives are consumed through play. The pass starts when granted and runs for 30 minutes, including time spent outside the game. It does not renew automatically.

Virtual items have no cash value and cannot be exchanged for money. Already-used consumables are not restored as new items. Deleting your Gridly account removes the inventory and progress associated with it. Contact support if a purchase has not arrived or something has gone wrong.

Apple processes App Store payments. For refund requests, visit Apple’s Report a Problem website. Nothing on this page excludes rights you have under applicable consumer law.

Connectivity and changes

Account syncing, leaderboards and purchases need an internet connection. Features, level balancing and game content may change in future updates. We will communicate material changes that affect your use of the service.
