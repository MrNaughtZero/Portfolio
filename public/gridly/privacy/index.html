Gridly Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 October 2026

Who is responsible for your information

Gridly is provided by Neil McNaught, who is responsible for the personal information described in this notice. For privacy questions or requests, contact hello@neilmcnaught.dev. Support is also available at Gridly Support.

Information used by Gridly

When you play as a guest, Gridly uses an identifier to connect your game progress and inventory to your installation. Game settings and some progress are stored on your device. Guest play is not necessarily anonymous to the services that keep your progress or handle purchases.

If you choose Sign in with Apple, we receive an account identifier and the information Apple shares with your permission, which may include your name and email address or an Apple relay address. We store your chosen player name and avatar, scores, progress, inventory and relevant game activity. We do not receive your Apple password.

When you make a purchase, Apple handles payment. Gridly and RevenueCat process product and transaction identifiers, purchase dates, purchase status and identifiers used to deliver the items to the correct player. We do not receive your full payment-card details.

Our service providers may process technical information needed to operate their services, such as IP addresses, device or app information, request logs and errors. If you contact support, we use your contact details and the information you send us to handle your request.

Why we use this information

We use account, game and purchase information to provide the features you request: saving progress, syncing your account, delivering purchases and showing leaderboard results. Our lawful basis for this processing is performance of our agreement with you.

We use limited technical information and transaction records to secure the service, investigate errors, prevent fraud and stop duplicate purchase fulfilment. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in operating a reliable and fair game. We use support correspondence to answer your requests and resolve problems. Where we must retain or disclose information to meet a legal obligation, we rely on that obligation.

Gridly does not include advertising SDKs or request permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites. We do not sell your personal information. We do not make solely automated decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

What other players can see

If you participate in leaderboards, other players can see your player name, avatar and leaderboard results, including scores and featured words. Your email address is not displayed on the leaderboard. Avoid using personal or sensitive information in your player name.

Services we use

We use the following providers to operate Gridly:

  • Supabase: account authentication, database and backend services.
  • RevenueCat: validation and management of in-app purchases.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, App Store distribution and payment processing.
  • Expo: app delivery and updates.

These providers receive the information needed for their services and may also process some information under their own privacy notices.

We may share information where necessary to comply with the law, respond to a valid legal request or protect users and the service.

International processing

Some providers may process information outside the United Kingdom. Where UK data protection rules require safeguards for a transfer, these can include a recognised adequacy decision or approved contractual protections. Contact us for information about where your data is processed and the safeguards that apply.

How long information is kept

We keep account information and saved progress while needed to provide your account. You can request deletion through the app. Some purchase, inventory-ledger and provider records remain after account deletion and require separate handling. We assess these records against the need to resolve purchase disputes, prevent duplicate fulfilment and meet applicable legal obligations. We keep technical logs and support correspondence for as long as needed to investigate incidents, answer requests and resolve outstanding issues, taking account of applicable legal obligations.

Deleting an account and requesting deletion

Open your account from Gridly's home screen, choose Delete account and confirm. This removes your account, profile, saved progress and associated inventory from the game's account system. It does not automatically erase every transaction record held by Gridly, Apple or RevenueCat, or refund purchases.

For data associated with guest play, purchase records or other privacy requests, contact hello@neilmcnaught.dev. We may ask for limited information to verify that a request relates to your data. Never send your password, verification codes or card details.

Your rights

Depending on your circumstances and applicable law, you may request access to your information, correction, deletion, restriction of processing or a portable copy. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests. These rights are subject to legal exceptions. Contact us using the address above to make a request.

You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at UK Information Commissioner’s Office, or your local data protection authority.

Children

Gridly does not require players to provide their date of birth. Parents and guardians should supervise account use and purchases on a child's device. If you believe a child has supplied personal information that needs to be removed, contact us so we can investigate and respond.

Updates

We may update this notice when the game or our processing changes. The current notice will be available at this URL with its effective date.
