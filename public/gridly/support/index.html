Gridly Support

Need a hand with Gridly?

For help with a purchase, your account or a problem while playing, contact hello@neilmcnaught.dev. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, Gridly version and a short description of what happened. A screenshot can help. Never send passwords, Apple verification codes or payment-card details.

Getting started

Swap two tiles to make words of four to six letters across a row or down a column. Words read from left to right or from top to bottom. Open the game menu to replay the tutorial for your current mode.

Classic rewards words with extra moves. Timed challenges you to score before the clock runs out. Story takes you through 50 levels, with up to three stars to earn on each level.

Keeping your progress

You can play as a guest. To keep your progress with an account, open the account button on the home screen and choose Sign in with Apple. Use the same Apple account when playing on another device. Choose a player name and avatar to appear on the leaderboards. Guest progress may be lost if you delete the app or change devices.

Purchases and missing items

Gridly offers optional power-ups, a Story lives refill and a 30-minute unlimited-lives pass. Prices are shown before you confirm with Apple. The unlimited-lives pass is a one-off purchase, not a subscription; its timer continues while you are away from the game.

If an item has not arrived, check your internet connection, reopen Gridly and make sure you are signed into the same Gridly account. Use Restore purchases in the shop where available. Consumable items that have already been used cannot be restored as new items. If you still need help, contact us with the item name and approximate purchase date. Please do not buy it again just to test it.

Apple handles payments and refund requests. You can request help with a charge at Apple’s Report a Problem website.

Deleting your account

Open your Gridly account from the home screen, choose Delete account and confirm. This removes your account, profile, saved progress and power-up inventory. It cannot be undone. Deleting an account does not automatically refund purchases. For privacy requests, or help with data associated with guest play or purchases, contact hello@neilmcnaught.dev.
